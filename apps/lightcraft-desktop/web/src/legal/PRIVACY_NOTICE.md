## What we receive

Ember does not collect personal data. Your photos, catalog, edits and settings stay on your computer. We receive no analytics, crash reports or account data.

Ember contacts the network only when you ask it to:

- Check for Updates downloads an update file from the update host. Like any web request, that host sees your IP address and the app's user-agent.
- Optional downloads, such as AI models, fetch files from the host named in the download dialog. That host sees your IP address.
- Links you open (Help, project and community pages) open in your web browser and are governed by those sites.
